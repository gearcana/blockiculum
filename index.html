What this is

BLOCKICULUM makes curriculum lessons as animated video. Each lesson teaches one idea at a time, builds it concretely before it is named, and gives the learner a real chance to answer before the answer arrives.

The first lesson

What does a number mean? Counting 1 to 5. Early mathematics, ages 3–5: the counting words 1 to 5, giving each object exactly one word, and why the last number you say is the answer.

How lessons are made

  • One idea per lesson, taught concretely before it is formalised.
  • Every visual has to teach something; decoration that competes with the subject is cut.
  • Accurate captions, and a real pause after every question.
  • Nothing is claimed in a title or thumbnail that the lesson does not deliver.
